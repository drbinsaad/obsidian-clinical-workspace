# Code of conduct

Clinical Workspace welcomes respectful, safety-conscious collaboration.

## Expected conduct

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique code and decisions rather than people.
- Respect different levels of clinical, technical, and accessibility expertise.
- Make room for correction when a workflow or privacy assumption is wrong.
- Protect confidential information and use synthetic examples throughout this
  public project.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. Real patient information is never acceptable in this repository,
regardless of intent or consent.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock threads,
reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct threatens people,
privacy, or the project.

Use GitHub's built-in content-reporting controls for abusive public content. For
a private report to the maintainers, open a private advisory through the
repository Security tab and begin the title with `Conduct:`. Do not include
patient information or other third-party confidential material in either route.

Maintainers should limit a report to those who need it, avoid conflicts of
interest, and explain the resulting action when doing so is safe. Retaliation
against a good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
